# First-use feedback — 2026-10-09 (Rut, quick pass)

Gut reaction from a short first session. A more detailed list will follow.

## Onboarding
- Starting experience was poor. Opening a folder was hard to find and do.

## Navigation
- Stepping to the next photo skips several images instead of going one at a time.

## Status / feedback
- Bottom-left indicator stays on "Analyzing" with no hint of what is being analyzed, how far along it is, or whether it is stuck.

## Editing
- Editing feels odd overall, and the controls are hard to understand.
- Slider range is too narrow. Lightroom lets you push highlights, shadows, and the rest much further.
- Edits are slow to apply.
- Good: mask detection was fast.

## AI edit
- Returns a wall of text with a single accept or reject. No per-change breakdown and no way to keep some changes and drop others.

## Quitting
- ⌘Q did nothing with a photo open in Develop. Had to force quit. (Cause: quit was refused while a Develop session was open, with only a status-bar line. Fixed 2026-10-10: quit now saves and closes the session itself.)

## UI polish
- Tooltip delay on buttons is too long. Names should appear faster on hover.
- Text spills over or gets cut off by the sidebar.

## Competitors
- Lightcraft and Photocraft feel much better optimised than Tessera right now. Worth a look at what they do on first open and on slider latency.

## Design direction (2026-10-10)
- Rut wants a more modern feel. Adobe is the thing to move away from, not copy. Inspiration: Luminar and Affinity for the editor; Apple, Arc and Linear for the overall product feel.
- This is modern software with agents at its core, and it has to feel that way: the agent is not a side panel bolted on, it is how the app works.
- Related earlier note: the AI edit result arrives as a wall of text with a single accept/reject. That is the opposite of agent-native.
