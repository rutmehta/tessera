# P11 detail display oracle feasibility — source/SDK review only

Original acceptance is visible detail, not explicitly callback latency. tools/orchestrate/wp/M2-58/brief.md:4 says “exact after settle within 200 ms”; its mandatory upstream tools/orchestrate/audits/perf/REPORT.md:197 says “final detail appears within 200 ms after ordinary non-denoise mouse-up fixture.” The same row requires detail-open drag p95 within10% of detail-closed and treats denoise accuracy separately. Thus the current coordination presentation plan has a sound basis for rejecting a ready callback as sole proof. A requirement to measure physical panel scanout/photons would be stronger than that original wording and should not be silently imposed.

Current DetailPreviewView.show (apps/mac/Sources/Tessera/Inspector/DetailPreviewView.swift:67 onward) copies the final IOSurface region into a CGImage, assigns CALayer contents with actions disabled and commits. It has no drawable presented-time callback. Copies are important: the displayed CGImage is independent of subsequent producer reuse. Neither ready callback, contents assignment, transaction completion, nor a display-link tick proves that the expected exact-detail pixels appeared.

A defensible compositor-level oracle is technically available in installed public ScreenCaptureKit APIs. SCStreamFrameInfoDisplayTime is documented as mach absolute time when the frame was displayed by the WindowServer, and SCFrameStatusComplete indicates a newly generated frame. Capture actual display content (visible, unoccluded test window), locate the detail ROI with documented scale/content/screen rectangles, and compare captured pixels with a discriminating expected final detail image. Do not use a desktop-independent window stream as proof that a physically occluded window was visible. No current Codex screenshot/capture tool exposed here promises per-frame WindowServer display timestamps, stream completeness, or this pixel-to-input causal join; a screenshot's tool-return time is not such an oracle.

Minimum future measurement design: warm/start capture before input; preserve final-input session/revision/crop identity and a monotonic timestamp at mouse-up; capture complete display frames with raw pixels, status and displayTime; prove the ROI corresponds to the exact settled output, not a label/marker alone or a stale/coarse image. Use a fixture/edit whose final ROI is distinguishable from preceding revisions. Color management, scaling and overlays must be controlled and a comparison calibrated against the same display/capture path; raw equality with the pre-compositor sRGB IOSurface cannot simply be assumed. Store exact renderer output separately to prove correctness. If comparison cannot discriminate final from earlier output, the latency sample is unavailable.

Convert mach absolute ticks using the timebase and compare to the same monotonic input clock; QuartzCore CABase.h documents CACurrentMediaTime as mach_absolute_time converted to seconds. A first matching captured frame gives an observed upper bound on visible-compositor availability. At ordinary60Hz capture, samples may be roughly16.7ms apart, but the SDK's requested minimum interval is not a guaranteed maximum gap. Retain last nonmatch/first match times and observed gaps; report an interval/upper bound rather than pretending exact first appearance. Callback delivery delay is irrelevant when displayTime is valid. A conservative upper bound plus timestamp/matching uncertainty at or below200ms can support the original threshold. A late first observation, stalls, invalid status, or dropped/missing frames cannot prove a failure occurred at the physical first appearance; an interval crossing200ms is inconclusive. ScreenCaptureKit does not prove physical panel scanout.

Permissions are unresolved, not presumed. CGPreflightScreenCaptureAccess documents a check for current-process capture access; CGRequestScreenCaptureAccess may prompt, and SCStreamErrorUserDeclined documents denied authorization. No permission check/request/capture was executed here. A fresh isolated helper or differently signed executable cannot inherit a claim based on another app's access. If capture is unavailable under already granted permissions, keep P11 display measurement pending and ask the coordinator for the specific prerequisite; do not install tools or request permission opportunistically.

Capture itself can add compositor/GPU work. Use the same warmed capture configuration in both paired detail-open/closed runs, retain load/configuration and uncertainties, and do not subtract guessed capture overhead. This note identifies a feasible route; it authorizes no new source changes or runtime and does not replace the existing measurement plan.
