# WP M2-41 — People view fixes: detail view overflow; launch layout glitch with --seed-faces

Two defects observed on screen (tools/orchestrate/wp/M2-40v2/verdict.json; screenshot tools/orchestrate/wp/M2-41/launch-broken.png):
1. **Person detail view overflows the window.** Double-clicking a person tile (`person-tile-<id>`) opens the detail view (apps/mac/Sources/Tessera/People/PeopleView.swift), but its content extends above the visible window: the top controls (name, counts, Show Photos, Confirm All) and first face chips are outside the window while the lower part is blank. The detail must be embedded in a scroll view anchored to the top, sized to the content area, and must never extend beyond the window. Check the NSHostingView/SwiftUI sizing (`fixedSize`, `frame(maxHeight:)`, `.infinity` alignment) and the container the People root view is placed into.

A later ⌘1 shows the normal grid. Reproduce (take window screenshots), find the cause (the People background refresh after folder load? the seeded-faces path? a stale window restoration?), fix it, and prove with before/after screenshots saved under tools/orchestrate/wp/M2-41/evidence/. Also check that the same launch without `--seed-faces` is unaffected.
Also: the People grid with 2 tiles leaves the content area mostly blank; make tiles flow left-to-right with a sensible width (like the photo grid) and the header full width, per DESIGN.md.
